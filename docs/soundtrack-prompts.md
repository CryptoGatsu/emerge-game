# Soundtrack prompts for Suno

Ambient loops for the game. Instrumental only, no vocals, so the world's own
sounds (rain, birds, the market) can sit on top. Ask for 3–4 minutes each and
a clean loop point. One prompt per track; the style line goes in Suno's style
field and the description in the prompt field.

## 1. Settlement — the plaza at midday

**Style:** ambient folk, acoustic, pastoral, lo-fi, instrumental, no vocals,
gentle, looping

**Prompt:** A quiet timber settlement at midday. Soft fingerpicked nylon
guitar, a wooden flute far off, a slow hand drum like footsteps on a dirt
lane, wind chimes now and then. Warm, unhurried, a little wistful. No melody
that demands attention; it should sit under a game for an hour without
wearing thin. Steady tempo around 72 BPM, no build, no drop, ends where it
began so it loops.

## 2. Settlement — dusk and the campfire

**Style:** ambient, acoustic, nocturne, warm drones, instrumental, no vocals,
slow

**Prompt:** Evening falling on a village. A held cello drone, a hurdy-gurdy
hum far away, occasional soft harp notes like sparks off a fire, a low bodhrán
pulse every few bars. Crickets implied by high shimmering pads. Melancholy but
safe, the feeling of a day's work done. Around 60 BPM, very sparse, loops
cleanly.

## 3. Winter — snow on the roofs

**Style:** ambient, minimal, piano, glassy pads, cold, instrumental, no
vocals, slow

**Prompt:** A settlement under snow. Sparse felted piano notes with long
decay, a soft bowed vibraphone, a wide slow pad like wind across a frozen
field. Very little movement. Cold and calm rather than sad. Around 56 BPM, no
percussion, long tails, seamless loop.

## 4. The market — trade day

**Style:** ambient folk, lively, marimba, hand percussion, instrumental, no
vocals, medium tempo, looping

**Prompt:** A market square filling up. Marimba and kalimba patterns
interlocking, a light frame-drum groove, a mandolin picking a cheerful figure
that never quite becomes a tune, a distant accordion. Busy but never loud.
Around 96 BPM, keeps a steady energy without a chorus, loops.

## 5. Danger — the storm coming

**Style:** dark ambient, cinematic, tension, low strings, taiko, instrumental,
no vocals, rising

**Prompt:** A tornado on the horizon of a small town. Low cello and bass
drones, a taiko hit every eight bars, a rising string swell that never fully
resolves, wind-like noise sweeps, distant metallic creaks. Ominous and wide,
not action music. Around 64 BPM. Must loop without a climax so it can run
until the danger passes.

## 6. Night — everybody asleep

**Style:** ambient, drone, night, very slow, music box, instrumental, no
vocals

**Prompt:** Two in the morning in a sleeping village. A single music-box
phrase that appears once a minute, a deep warm sub drone, the faintest
high-frequency shimmer like stars. Almost nothing happens. Around 50 BPM,
tender, loops seamlessly.

## 7. The world map — choosing land

**Style:** ambient, orchestral, wonder, strings and woodwinds, instrumental,
no vocals, slow, expansive

**Prompt:** Looking down on an archipelago from above. Slow layered strings,
a solo oboe line that wanders, soft timpani rolls under the swells, a harp
arpeggio when the strings rest. Hopeful, spacious, the feeling of an
unclaimed world. Around 66 BPM, no drums, gentle rises and falls, loops.

## 8. Visiting — somebody else's town

**Style:** ambient, curious, dulcimer, clarinet, light, instrumental, no
vocals, medium-slow

**Prompt:** Walking into a town you did not build. Hammered dulcimer patterns,
a warm clarinet melody with room to breathe, a soft brushed snare, a plucked
bass keeping time. Friendly and a little curious. Around 84 BPM, steady, no
big changes, loops.

## Notes for later eras

When the game advances past the settlement era, the same eight moods repeat
with different instruments: Township adds brass and a church bell; Industrial
adds mechanical rhythms, steam hiss and a distant whistle; Modern adds
electric piano, soft synth bass and traffic-like white noise; the AI era goes
to pure synthesis, granular pads and a very slow pulse. Keep the tempos and
the loop rule.
